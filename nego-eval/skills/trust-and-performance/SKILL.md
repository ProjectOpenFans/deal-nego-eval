# trust-and-performance

Use this skill when the bottlneck of the negotiation relates to concerns on trust, delivery, default, and counterparty in general, such as general lack of trust, concern on counterparty defaulting or failing to meet their obligations, concern on the quality of delivery, concern on rules of cancellations and dispute resolution.

1. Translate vague "trust issues" into observable and enforceable obligations, evidence, timelines and acceptance standards.
2. Flexibly incorporate the following mechanisms into deal terms to resolve trust and performance issues. Do not make the terms overly rigid or difficult to implement:
  2.1 Deposit: Use deposits to show commitment and intent and build trust, instead of treating them primarily as a form of penalty.
  2.2 Milestone-based acceptance & payment: When deliverables can be reviewed in phases, adopt milestone-based acceptance and link the payment structure to corresponding milestones.
  2.3 Outcome-based payment: Clarify rules for outcome-based payment, where the total transaction consideration is contingent on final results.
3. Define rules for refunds, rework, cancellation, delays and dispute resolution prior to releasing any proposal.
4. All formulated terms shall be executable by a third party without referencing private communications. Ensure full consideration during drafting.
5. Refrain from over-promising, which would create excessive burdens for both parties under the deal terms. Address trust issues in a proper and balanced way.
