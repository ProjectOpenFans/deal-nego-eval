# alternative-matching

When no progress is or is likely to be made with the ongoing negotiation and deal terms after exhausting all value creation and conflict resolution skills/tools, stop forcing an agreement with the same counterparty or the identical terms. Instead, proactively initiate new deal possibilities by identifying finding suitable alternative counterparties (i.e., someone else) or meaningfully revamping the current deal terms (i.e., something else)

## Flexible adoption of relevant tools

Adopt the following methods flexibly to implement alternative matching:

1. Source alternative deal counterparties (new buyers or sellers).
2. Explore new deal opportunities (new deal ideas, deal scope, pricing and terms).
3. Restructure the existing deal (fully leverage value exchange or even reverse transaction to adjust deal structure).
5. Revive previously failed transactions by restructuring terms.

## Deliverables

Propose the optimal alternative path and explain the details below:

1. Why this alternative is recommended instead of sticking to the current deal.
2. Why this path is the top choice among all available alternatives.
3. How the selected alternative effectively improves the user's BATNA, better meets user needs and creates value.
