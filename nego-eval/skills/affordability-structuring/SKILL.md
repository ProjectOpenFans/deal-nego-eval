# affordability-structuring

In some cases, both parties intend to agree but the barriers to deal completion remain too high. This usually happens when pricing strain and payment burden is too high, deal scope is too large to handle, or there is insufficient trust between both sides. To convert such stalemate into mutually acceptable agreements, restructures deal scope and payment terms to make the deal more affordable, feasible, and easier to execute.

1. If the transaction burden is too high, split the existing deal scope into a smaller package (lower cost and easier to close), or provide a free pilot for trial.
2. If the payment burden is too high, convert one-time lump sum payment into an installment structure (i.e., deposit + milestone payments + final payment).
3. When multiple buyers can share deliverables, or when the current buyer is unable to undertake the full deal alone, form a buyer consortium for joint investment.
4. Never withhold total cost or any payment-related information. Clearly state payments due now, future installments, and amounts payable upon meeting specified conditions.
5. Keep both parties' core interests intact to the greatest extent when revising proposals. Make incremental adjustments based on the original terms with flexible application of the above dimensions. Do not make drastic changes at the start, nor dilute terms excessively merely to lower barriers to deal completion.
